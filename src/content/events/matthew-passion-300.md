This session at the 2026 Bach Network Dialogue Meeting celebrates the 300th anniversary (give or take) of the first performance of J. S. Bach's Matthew Passion, hosted by Bettina Varwig, with guests Michael Marissen, Margaret Faultless, Nicholas Mulroy and Andrew Skidmore. We will be talking and playing through a selection of key moments in the piece and think through the broader historical, theological and performance practice issues that they raise.
