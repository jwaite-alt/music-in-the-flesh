Gospel Oak Chorale is a group of established amateur and young professional singers giving quarterly public choral concerts in London, actively exploring ideas of embodiment in musical performance pioneered by Bettina Varwig and John Landor.

The choir is community focused: we encourage young people, students and locals with some background in choral singing to collaborate over a weekend of open workshops that culminate in a free public concert. We work to broaden access both to the traditions of choral singing and the reinvention of its performance, including instrumental arrangements, that is at once historically informed and improvisational. In this sense we see ourselves as a development of Varwig's and Landor's work and aim to bring their principles to a wider constituency of performers and audiences.

Our unticketed concerts take place at All Hallows Church in London NW3. We collect donations for the London Churches Refugee Fund, and seek small awards from institutional funders, enabling us to provide stipends for student and early career professional singers and musicians to attend rehearsals and performance weekends. Our intent is twofold: both to encourage greater experimentation amongst the choir as a whole with the benefit of more experienced support, and to expose our visiting singers to the methods and techniques we are developing. We intend that their time with us will go on to inform their future practice as choral singers in teaching and leading capacities.
