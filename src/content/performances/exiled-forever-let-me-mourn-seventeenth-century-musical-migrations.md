This year's Baroque Week theme was Northward Bound: A Musical Voyage around Northern Europe and the Baltics. This talk by Bettina Varwig explored music along the early modern Hanseatic routes connected with themes of exile, migration, loss and longing, with a selection of repertoire performed by participants.
