In this day-long workshop we explored different ways of being in the body musically through Bach's monumental Passion setting. We talked about hearts and tears, pulse, dissonance, leadership and dance, and ended up with a full tutti rendition of the aria 'Mache Dich, mein Herze, rein' - a deeply moving demonstration of the power of affective contagion.
